    "Contributors\n",
    "============\n",
    "CRO grew out of the [CLIVAR ENSO Conceptual Model Working Group](https://www.clivar.org/enso-conceptual-model-working-group) and its collaborative review paper, [The El Niño Southern Oscillation (ENSO) Recharge Oscillator Conceptual Model: Achievements and Future Prospects](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2024RG000843). The review synthesized the development and applications of the Recharge Oscillator and highlighted the need for an open-access, community-standard implementation of the model. Building directly on this effort, the Community Recharge Oscillator (CRO) was subsequently developed as an open-source Python and MATLAB framework for configuring and solving a broad family of Recharge Oscillator models, estimating their parameters, and supporting reproducible ENSO research and teaching.\n",
